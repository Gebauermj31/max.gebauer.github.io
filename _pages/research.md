<div class="research-grid">
  <section class="research-panel">
    <span class="meta-label">Pillar 01</span>
    <h3>Philosophy of science &amp; Bayesianism</h3>
    <p>I examine how epistemic and social values enter scientific practice, with particular attention to Bayesian inference, inductive risk, and decisions under uncertainty.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="research-panel">
    <span class="meta-label">Pillar 02</span>
    <h3>Statistical methodology</h3>
    <p>I am interested in model selection, calibration, causal control selection, hierarchical structure, and validation designs that preserve the distinction between tuning and honest evaluation.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="research-panel">
    <span class="meta-label">Pillar 03</span>
    <h3>Applied research</h3>
    <p>I apply those commitments in sports, climate, and health—including public work on <a href="{{ '/projects/contextual-xwoba/' | relative_url }}">contextual xwOBA</a>, confidential sports research, and spatial epidemiology.</p>
  </section>
</div>
